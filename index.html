Fleet Encrypted Backups

A personal desktop utility that backs up selected local files to the owner's Google Drive. It uses restic to encrypt backup contents and rclone to transfer the encrypted repository.

Purpose and access

The application creates and accesses its own backup files and folders through the Google Drive drive.file permission. It does not request access to the entire Drive. The desktop client is named Fleet Storage Backup.

The owner authorizes Google access in Google's consent flow. The utility can upload backups, read repository metadata, download backup data for verification, and restore selected files. It is operated privately for its owner and does not offer public account registration.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for the data used, storage, retention, and how to revoke access. For questions, use the developer support email displayed on the Google consent screen.

This website describes the application. It has no sign-in form and does not receive Google authorization tokens.
